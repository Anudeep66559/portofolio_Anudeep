S-02

General Notes

Profile · Education · Leadership
Sheet 02 of 06 · Rev A

I am a B.Tech Civil Engineering undergraduate at IIT Hyderabad, with a strong foundation in structural mechanics, construction materials and computational methods. My path is shaped by a curiosity for how things are designed, built and made to last — from reinforced concrete frames to sustainable cement blends and 3D-printed models.

During a three-month internship at Nikko Co., Ltd. in Japan, I worked on sustainable concrete mix design using GGBS as a high-volume cement replacement, studying flowability and mixer power consumption. On campus I lead student-support and alumni-engagement initiatives, and have built projects in STAAD.Pro, MATLAB image processing, CAD and responsive web design.

"Where the structure meets the signal — drafted, analysed, built."

I move comfortably between the drawing board, the laboratory and the code editor — reading standards, running analyses, building prototypes and shipping interfaces. I am open to opportunities across civil engineering and technology, and particularly drawn to frontend developer and software / tech roles where I can turn ideas into fast, accessible products. Wherever I land, I bring engineering rigour and a habit of building from the ground up.

FIG 1.1

Structural Analysis · RC Design · STAAD.Pro · IS 456 · SP 34

Modelled and analysed a multi-span reinforced concrete frame in STAAD.Pro under gravity loading and load combinations, examining member forces and critical load paths.

Designed one-way slab, three-span beam and columns for flexure, shear and combined axial load–moment effects, with reinforcement detailing per IS 456 and SP 34.

Developed a MATLAB image-processing system to track sediment particle movement in supercritical flows (Fr > 1), converting frame sequences into particle trajectories.
